How this grows

The three simulators stay independent and independently testable — their repositories remain the source of truth for each engine. What gets built here, in this repository, is the integration between them:

  • Planned: shared resource accounting — a solar-powered water pump drawing from the energy budget, livestock water demand drawing from storage.
  • Planned: a shared simulation clock and data contracts (quantities, units, timestamps, provenance, uncertainty) so the three engines can exchange numbers without changing each other's physics.
  • Planned: unified scenarios, reports, and a shared shopping/planning layer for equipment.

Nothing on this page claims integration that does not exist yet. Each discipline keeps its own validation: electrical, water treatment, and biological models are not validated by identical tests.
